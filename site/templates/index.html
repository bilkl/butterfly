Change one value. The rest follows.

Butterfly is a language where a cell can always be equal to a formula, as in a spreadsheet. When a source changes, everything that depends on it updates by itself, exactly once and in the right order.

    Three words to remember

    soit, a source

    A cell you can change whenever you like.

    soit prix = 20;
prix = 25;

    lie, a derived cell

    A cell that is always equal to its formula. You never assign it a value.

    lie total = prix * 3;

    quand, an observer

    A block that runs every time the value of a cell changes.

    quand total {
    affiche total;
}

    A small budget

    The expenses, the remainder and the rate are derived cells. Every time a source changes, the observer on reste ("remainder") reacts, without a single line of recomputation being written. The output beside it is the real compiler's.
